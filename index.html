Quality rules — fixed, always enforced
  • Published emails only. Copied from a public source. Never guessed, never pattern-built (no invented info@ or firstname@ addresses).
  • Multi-source verification. Never settle for the first pass — official sites, press, directories and review platforms cross-checked until the realistic ceiling is reached.
  • Evidence on every field. A source URL and an evidence type stand behind every researched value.
  • Deduped. One row per unique email; shared inboxes flagged so nobody gets pitched twice.
  • Suppression respected. Dead domains, double failures and do-not-contact addresses go to the Suppression register, never back into the list.

The master research prompt

How to use: copy this prompt, paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Codex, and run it. The AI does the research legwork and returns tab-ready tables. Bring those tables back here, to the Results Viewer, and export them as a workbook-grade spreadsheet.

The verification loop: one AI researches and fills the sheet — then a different AI gets read + write access to the same sheet to independently verify it: are the published emails real and backed by their source URLs, do the Instagram handles exist and match the business, did duplicates or suppressed addresses slip in? The checker writes its findings back into the record (Checked date, status fields, Notes, and an Activity log entry under its own name). For this to work, the generated Google Sheet must be shared with edit access to the account the verifying AI uses.

The prompt encodes the Super Intelligence research standard, the gold-standard workbook architecture (About · Contacts · Directory · Held contacts · Coverage · Activity log · Suppression register), Instagram verification labels, and the rule that published — never guessed — emails are the only emails allowed.

Results — your CRM desk

Paste the Contacts table an AI platform returned (CSV), load the built-in sample, or open a CSV file. Search it, filter it, and export it again in the gold-standard Contacts format.

Exports use the gold-standard Contacts columns — the same architecture as the reference workbooks — so a Merlin export drops straight into a spreadsheet next to the About, Directory, Held contacts, Coverage, Activity log and Suppression register tabs described in TEMPLATE.md.

Merlin performs no scraping itself. Merlin writes the brief; the AI platform you choose runs the research; Merlin receives, checks and presents the results. The workbook architecture is modelled on two reference implementations — a pair of ski-resort outreach workbooks (US and Canada editions) built to this same standard.
